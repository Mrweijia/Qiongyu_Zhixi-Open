## Key takeaways

- PM2.5 model gains widen with forecast horizon: ΔR² is approximately +0.006 at 1h and +0.275 at 24h.
- At 24h, PM2.5 MAE falls from 15.14 to 12.86 µg/m³ versus persistence.
- The all-model results show positive ΔR² for every pollutant/horizon cell in the provided summary.
- At 24h, all nine PM2.5 monitoring sites show positive site-level ΔR².
